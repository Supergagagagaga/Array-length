Sun tracking (AR)

Open the camera and look around. The sun, its path for the day, nearby buildings and trees, and their shadows on the ground are drawn over the live picture.

Never look at the sun directly, and do not stare at it through the camera. Look at the screen, not the sun.

Getting an accurate overlay
  1. Settle the compass. Wave the phone in a figure 8 a few times, away from cars, switchboards and steel.
  2. Align to sun. Point the camera near the sun (look at the screen, not the sun) and tap Lock on sun. The app finds the sun in the picture and fixes the compass. Do this again if you move somewhere new.
  3. Check another time. Drag the time slider to see where the sun and the shadows will be.
  4. Mark obstacles. Tap where a wall or trunk meets the ground. A magnifier helps you place the point. The blue model should sit on the real building before you save.
